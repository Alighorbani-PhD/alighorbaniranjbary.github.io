About

I am a molecular biology researcher currently affiliated with the Department of Immunology at the University of Tehran, Iran. My work focuses on immunology and the tumor microenvironment, cell signaling, apoptosis, metastasis, cancer research, and immunosurveillance.

I also specialize in cloning and genetic engineering techniques including CRISPR-Cas9/Cas13a, T-cell engineering, PD-1/PD-L1 signaling pathways, lentiviral transduction, and genome research.
