About me

I am a Molecular Biology Researcher in the Department of Immunology at the University of Tehran, Iran, with a DVM and Ph.D. background and research experience in cancer biology, immunology and molecular engineering.

My research interests include immunology and the tumor microenvironment, cell signaling, apoptosis, metastasis, immunosurveillance and DNA mismatch repair. I also work with cloning and engineering approaches including CRISPR-Cas systems, T-cell engineering and lentiviral transduction.
